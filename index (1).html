How to read this

Each trait is scored from 4 to 20. Scores here are labeled lower (4 to 9), middle (10 to 14), or higher (15 to 20) on this scale. They are not compared with a national sample, so treat them as a starting point for discussion. This is a short learning tool, not a hiring test. It describes tendencies, not ability or worth, and no score is the best one.

Items are from the Mini-IPIP, a 20-item short form drawn from the International Personality Item Pool (Donnellan, Oswald, Baird and Lucas, 2006). The item pool is in the public domain: ipip.ori.org.
